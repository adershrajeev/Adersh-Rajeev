How It Works

A smart 3-step process powered by Fuzzy Logic

Enter Specifications

Input your mobile phone's specifications — RAM, Battery, Camera, Storage, Processor Speed, and more through a user-friendly form.

Fuzzy Inference Engine

Your inputs are processed through a Fuzzy Logic system that applies expert rules using membership functions and fuzzy reasoning to evaluate the price tier.

Receive an instant prediction — Budget, Mid-Range, Premium, or Flagship — along with confidence level, price range, and export options.
